FAQ

Common questions

How much does an automation cost?

It depends on what the automation needs to do. After a free audit call you get a fixed quote, so you know the price before any work starts.

How long does it take?

Small automations can often be ready within days. Larger systems take a few weeks. You get a timeline together with the quote.

Is my data safe?

Where possible I build on your own accounts, use secure connections and only give the workflow access to what it needs. You keep ownership of everything.

Which tools do I need?

Usually the ones you already use. I work with n8n, Make.com, Claude and most apps that offer an API or webhooks.

What happens if something breaks?

Workflows include error alerts and come with documentation. Monthly support is available if you want me to keep an eye on things.
